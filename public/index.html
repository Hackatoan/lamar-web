Commands

How to talk to him.

All commands use the lamar prefix. Or just mention him directly for AI chat.

@Lamar [message]
AI conversation — he responds in character with memory of past messages
lamar help
List all available commands
lamar attack @user
Attack another member Lamar-style
lamar rps [rock|paper|scissors]
Challenge Lamar to rock paper scissors
lamar story 1
Hear one of Lamar's stories
lamar nword
You know what this does
lamar die
Make Lamar... meet his end
lamar shoot @user
Shoot someone (in-bot, relax)
